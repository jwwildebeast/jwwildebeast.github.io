# John Wayne Williams

Ph.D. candidate and Graduate Research Assistant in Industrial & Systems Engineering at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville.

[Website](https://jwwildebeast.github.io) • [CV](https://jwwildebeast.github.io/cv/) • [Publications](https://jwwildebeast.github.io/publications/) • [Talks](https://jwwildebeast.github.io/talks/) • [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=PS_CX0AAAAAJ) • [ORCID](https://orcid.org/0009-0005-8169-209X)

I research the intersection of human factors, artificial intelligence, and systems engineering. My work focuses on LLM-based agents, human-AI collaboration, digital twins, and decision support for complex industrial and sociotechnical systems.

## About

This repository hosts the source for my academic and professional portfolio. It includes my research profile, publications, talks, CV, and project work, and is built with Jekyll using a customized Academic Pages template.

The original template documentation is preserved in [docs/academic-pages-template.md](docs/academic-pages-template.md).

## Repository structure

- `_pages/` — main site pages and profile content
- `_publications/` — research publications and articles
- `_talks/` — talks, presentations, and demos
- `_portfolio/` — project and portfolio entries
- `files/` — downloadable materials, PDFs, and supporting documents
- `_data/` — site metadata and author settings
- `_config.yml` — site configuration
