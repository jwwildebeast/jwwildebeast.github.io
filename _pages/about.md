I am a Ph.D. candidate and Graduate Research Assistant in the Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville, working in the Applied Systems Laboratory (ASL). I began my research at UT in 2023 in the Ideation Laboratory (iLab) under Dr. Xueping Li and moved to the ASL in 2024. I expect to complete my Ph.D. in late 2027.

My research combines human factors, artificial intelligence, and systems engineering. I build LLM-based agents and multi-agent systems for complex reasoning and decision-making, study how AI assistants affect human situational awareness and team performance, and apply digital twins, machine learning, and agent-based simulation to industrial and sociotechnical systems. I am especially interested in trustworthy, human-centered AI that supports rather than replaces human judgment in complex, safety-critical environments.

## Research Interests

- **LLM agents and multi-agent systems**: designing and evaluating agents for complex reasoning, planning, and decision-making in industrial contexts.
- **Human-AI collaboration**: how AI assistants and agent design shape situational awareness, trust, and team dynamics, including the role of personality.
- **Digital twins and predictive maintenance**: machine learning and LLMs for monitoring, diagnosing, and maintaining industrial equipment.
- **Simulation and optimization**: agent-based modeling of sociotechnical systems, optimization, graph and network methods, and parallel computing.

## Highlights

- **Commercialized research software.** I led development of an XR digital twin for industrial motor predictive maintenance. The University of Tennessee has copyrighted the software and is licensing it to industry partners for commercialization. [Read more](/portfolio/xr-digital-twin/).
- **Publications.** Two journal articles in *Computers & Industrial Engineering* (one as first author) and papers at the Winter Simulation Conference, the IISE Annual Conference, and the International Conference on Computers and Industrial Engineering. [See all publications](/publications/).
- **Demonstrations.** Live demos of the digital twin at MARCON 2026 and at the XR Symposium (ORTECC) in 2025 and 2026. [See talks and demos](/talks/).

## Background

I hold a B.S. in Computer Science (Software Engineering concentration) from Colorado Technical University, summa cum laude, and an M.S. in Industrial and Systems Engineering from UT, earned concurrently with my Ph.D. Before graduate school, I spent seven years as a store manager at FedEx Office and four years in medical coding and billing.

I am a member of ACM, INFORMS, IISE, and Upsilon Pi Epsilon.
