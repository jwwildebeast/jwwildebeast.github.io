I am a Ph.D. student and Graduate Research Assistant in the Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville. I began my research at UT in 2023 in the Ideation Laboratory (iLab) under Dr. Xueping Li and transitioned to the Applied Systems Laboratory (ASL) in 2024. My current work focuses on human-AI collaboration and complex systems modeling.

## Research Focus

My research centers on the intersection of human factors, artificial intelligence, and systems engineering:

- **Personality and AI**: Examining how personality traits shape human-AI team dynamics and AI agent design.
- **Large Language Model Agents**: Developing and evaluating LLM-based agents for complex reasoning and decision-making in industrial contexts.
- **Agent-Based Simulation**: Applying computational simulation to model emergent behavior in sociotechnical systems.

I am interested in developing trustworthy, human-centered AI systems that augment rather than replace human judgment in complex, safety-critical environments.

This site highlights my publications, research talks, teaching, and selected project work.

## Current Projects

- **graphmem**: A memory layer for large language models that supports shared context across models, memory archival, and structured note-taking.
- **CortexRuntime**: A runtime and orchestration framework for large language model systems in which a planner model decomposes missions into subtasks and coordinating agents bid based on resources, historical performance, and capabilities.
- **SituationalAwarenessStudy**: An ongoing study investigating how AI assistants influence situational awareness, including experimental design and evaluation.
