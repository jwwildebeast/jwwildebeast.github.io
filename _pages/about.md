I am a Ph.D. candidate and Graduate Research Assistant in the Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville. I began my doctoral work in 2023 in the Ideation Laboratory (iLab) under Dr. Xueping Li and later joined the Applied Systems Laboratory (ASL), where I continue to study how intelligent systems can better support human judgment in complex environments.

My research sits at the intersection of human factors, artificial intelligence, and systems engineering. I build LLM-based agents and multi-agent systems for reasoning and decision support, study how AI assistants shape situational awareness and team performance, and apply digital twins, machine learning, and simulation to industrial and sociotechnical systems.

I am particularly interested in trustworthy, human-centered AI systems that augment rather than replace expert decision making in high-stakes domains.

## Research focus

- **LLM agents and multi-agent systems**: designing and evaluating agents for planning, reasoning, and decision support in industrial settings.
- **Human-AI collaboration**: understanding how AI assistants influence trust, team dynamics, and situational awareness.
- **Digital twins and predictive maintenance**: using machine learning and LLMs to monitor, diagnose, and maintain industrial equipment.
- **Simulation and optimization**: developing agent-based and computational models for complex sociotechnical systems.

## Selected highlights

- **Commercialized research software.** I led development of an XR digital twin for industrial motor predictive maintenance, which the University of Tennessee has copyrighted and made available for licensing to industry partners. [Read more](/portfolio/xr-digital-twin/)
- **Published work.** My research includes journal articles in *Computers & Industrial Engineering* and conference papers in simulation, industrial engineering, and applied AI. [See all publications](/publications/)
- **Live demonstrations.** I have presented digital twin and AI-enabled systems work at MARCON 2026 and the XR Symposium (ORTECC) in 2025 and 2026. [See talks and demos](/talks/)

## Background

I hold a B.S. in Computer Science (Software Engineering concentration) from Colorado Technical University, summa cum laude, and an M.S. in Industrial and Systems Engineering from the University of Tennessee. Before graduate school, I spent seven years as a store manager at FedEx Office and four years in medical coding and billing.

I am a member of ACM, INFORMS, IISE, and Upsilon Pi Epsilon.
