I am a PhD student and graduate research assistant in the Department of Industrial and Systems Engineering at the University of Tennessee, Knoxville. I work in the Applied Systems Laboratory under the supervision of advisors focused on human-AI collaboration and complex systems modeling.

## Research Focus

My research explores the intersection of human factors, artificial intelligence, and systems engineering:

- **Personality and AI**: Understanding how personality traits influence human-AI team dynamics and AI agent design
- **Large Language Model Agents**: Developing and evaluating LLM-based agents for complex reasoning and decision-making in industrial contexts
- **Agent-Based Simulation**: Using computational simulation to model emergent behaviors in sociotechnical systems

I am interested in building trustworthy, human-centered AI systems that augment rather than replace human judgment in complex, safety-critical environments.

This site is where I share publications, research talks, teaching, and selected project work.
